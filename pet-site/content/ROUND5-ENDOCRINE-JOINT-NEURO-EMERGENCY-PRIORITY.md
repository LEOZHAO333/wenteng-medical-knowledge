- 糖尿病：2026 AAHA Diabetes Management Guidelines for Cats / Dogs。

- 慢性关节痛：2022 AAHA Pain Management Guidelines；2023 AAHA Senior Care Guidelines。
- 抽搐与癫痫：Merck Veterinary Manual Epilepsy in Small Animals；2024 ACVIM cluster seizure/status epilepticus consensus。
- 中毒与误食：Merck Veterinary Manual Food Hazards、Xylitol、Chocolate、Grape/Raisin、Poisonous Plants 等。
